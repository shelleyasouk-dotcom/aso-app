# ASO Coaching App — Coach Guide

Hey! Welcome to the ASO app. This is where you clock in and out, track your expenses, and keep your coaching ID up to date. It takes about 5 minutes to set up and is super easy to use day-to-day.

---

## Step 1 — Get the App on Your Phone

The app works in your phone browser but feels way better saved to your home screen (it opens like a normal app).

**iPhone — must use Safari:**
1. Open your app link in **Safari**
2. Tap the share button at the bottom (the box with the arrow)
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

**Android — use Chrome:**
1. Open the link in **Chrome**
2. Tap the three dots at the top right
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**

Done — you'll see the ASO icon on your home screen.

---

## Step 2 — Log In

- Your **email** and **password** were sent to you by your Area Lead
- Tap **Change my password** from the home screen after logging in to set something you'll remember

> Keep your login details safe — your account has personal information and your DBS details on it.

---

## Clocking In and Out

This is the main thing you'll use every session. Please don't forget — it's how your hours are tracked.

**To clock in:**
1. Tap **Clock In / Out** on the home screen
2. Select your school from the dropdown (or it auto-selects if you only work at one)
3. Tap the big **Clock In** button
4. You'll see a green confirmation message

**To clock out:**
1. Open the app again after your session
2. Tap the big red **Clock Out** button

That's it! The app shows you the current time so you can see exactly when you're clocking in.

> **Forgot to clock out?** Don't worry — message your Area Lead and they can fix it for you.

---

## Your Profile and Digital ID

Your profile is your digital coaching ID. It's important to keep it updated because it shows your DBS status and certificate expiry dates.

**To set up your profile:**
1. Tap **My Profile** on the home screen
2. Tap **Upload Photo** — choose a clear photo of your face (like a passport photo style)
3. Fill in your details:
   - Phone number
   - DBS Certificate Number (on your DBS certificate)
   - DBS Expiry Date
   - Safeguarding Certificate Expiry
   - First Aid Certificate Expiry
4. Tap **Save Details**

Your digital ID card will appear at the top of your profile — you can show this on your phone if a school asks for it.

**Adding certificates and qualifications:**
1. On your profile page, scroll to **Certificates & Qualifications**
2. Tap **Add**
3. Enter the certificate name (e.g. UKAG Level 2, Safeguarding Awareness)
4. Add the date issued and expiry date
5. Optionally upload a photo of the certificate
6. Tap **Add**

## Submitting Expenses

If you spend money on travel for ASO sessions, you can claim it back through the app. Your Area Lead will then approve or reject it.

**To submit an expense:**
1. Tap **Expenses** on the home screen
2. Tap **Submit Expense**
3. Choose the type:

| Type | Use for |
|------|---------|
| **Mileage** | Driving to sessions in your own car |
| **Travel** | Train, bus, parking, taxi |
| **Other** | Anything else — keep the receipt |

4. Fill in the date and a description (e.g. "Drive to St Peter's School, Winchester")
5. For **mileage**: enter the number of miles — the amount calculates itself at 45p per mile
6. For **travel/other**: enter the amount you spent
7. Tap **Submit**

You'll see the status on your Expenses page:
- 🟡 **Pending** — waiting for your Area Lead to review
- 🟢 **Approved** — you'll be paid this
- 🔴 **Rejected** — your Area Lead may have left a note explaining why

## Session Registers

If you're a Lead Coach or above, you can take attendance registers for your sessions.

1. Tap **Session Register** on the home screen
2. Tap **New Register**
3. Select your school and session date
4. Mark each child as present or absent
5. Tap **Submit**

## Documents

All ASO policies, handbooks, and forms are in the Documents section.

1. Tap **Documents** from the home screen
2. Browse by category or scroll through
3. Tap the download icon on any document to open it

---

## Announcements

Your Area Lead and management post important updates here — things like session changes, reminders, or policy updates.

They appear automatically on your home screen. Pinned messages stay at the top until they're removed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**I forgot my password — what do I do?**
Tap **Change my password** at the bottom of the home screen (you need to be logged in). If you can't log in at all, message your Area Lead.

**I forgot to clock out — what do I do?**
Message your Area Lead with the time you finished. They can edit the record for you.

**My school isn't showing in the clock in screen — what do I do?**
Your Area Lead needs to assign you to that school. Message them and they'll sort it quickly.

**I uploaded the wrong photo — how do I change it?**
Go to My Profile and tap **Change Photo** — it replaces the old one straight away.

**My DBS is about to expire — what should I do?**
The app will show a warning on your ID card when expiry is getting close. Flag it to your Area Lead as soon as possible.

**The app looks weird or isn't loading properly — what do I do?**
Close it and reopen it. If that doesn't work, delete it from your home screen and reinstall it (your data is saved — you won't lose anything).
