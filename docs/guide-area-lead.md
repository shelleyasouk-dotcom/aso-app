# ASO Coaching App — Area Lead Guide

Hey! 👋 This guide walks you through everything you need to know about the ASO app as an Area Lead. It's quicker than it looks — most things take just a few taps.

---

## First Things First — Get the App on Your Phone

The app works in any browser but feels way better when saved to your home screen.

**iPhone:**
1. Open the link in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the share icon at the bottom (box with arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add** — done!

**Android:**
1. Open the link in **Chrome**
2. Tap the three dots at the top right
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**

Now it'll open like a normal app.

---

## Logging In

- Use the **email and password** your director gave you
- If you forgot your password, tap **Change my password** from the home screen once you're in, or ask your director to reset it

---

## Your Home Screen

When you log in you'll see your personal home screen. As an Area Lead you get access to more tiles than regular coaches:

| Tile | What it's for |
|------|--------------|
| Clock In / Out | Record your own sessions |
| Timesheets | View and fix clock records for your area's coaches |
| Documents | View policies, handbooks, and forms |
| My Profile | Your digital ID card and certificates |
| Expenses | Submit your own travel and mileage |
| Admin Panel | Manage your area's schools, staff, and more |

---

## Your Admin Panel

Tap **Admin Panel** to get to your management tools:

- **Announcements** — post messages to your coaches
- **Area Schools** — view all schools in your area with their coaches
- **Staff** — manage your coaches
- **Children** — manage children at your schools
- **Expenses** — approve or reject expense claims from your coaches
- **Documents** — view and upload documents

---

## Posting an Announcement to Your Coaches

Got something important to share? Post an announcement — it appears on every coach's home screen as soon as they open the app.

1. Tap **Admin Panel → Announcements**
2. Tap **New Announcement**
3. Write your title and message
4. Set the **Audience** to your area (e.g. Hampshire)
5. Optionally add a link — like a Google Form, document, or anything useful
6. Toggle **Pin** if it's something they need to see every time they open the app
7. Tap **Post**

> Use this for: session reminders, policy updates, weather cancellations, anything important.

---

## Managing Your Coaches

### Viewing Your Coaches
1. Tap **Admin Panel → Staff**
2. Your coaches are grouped by area

### Assigning a Coach to a School
When a new coach joins or changes schools:
1. Find them in Staff and tap **Schools**
2. Tick all the schools they work at
3. Tap **Save**

This is important — it controls which schools appear when they clock in.

### Editing a Coach's Details
1. Find them in Staff and tap **Edit**
2. You can change their name, email, role, or area
3. You can also toggle **Can clock in anywhere** — use this for coaches who sometimes cover locations that aren't in the system

### Viewing a Coach's Profile
1. Find them in Staff and tap **Profile**
2. You'll see their digital ID card, DBS number, and certificate expiry dates
3. You can edit these on their behalf if they need help filling things in

---

## Timesheets — Fixing Clock Records

Sometimes coaches forget to clock in or out. You can fix these.

1. Tap **Timesheets** from the home screen
2. Use the filter at the top to find a specific coach
3. **To fix an incorrect time:** tap the pencil icon on the record
4. **To delete a wrong record:** tap the bin icon
5. **To add a missing record:** tap **Add Missing Clock Record** at the top, then select the coach, school, and times

---

## Approving Expenses

When a coach submits a travel or mileage claim, it lands here for you to review.

1. Tap **Admin Panel → Expenses**
2. The banner at the top tells you how many are waiting and the total value
3. Tap any expense to see the details
4. Add an optional note and tap **Approve** or **Reject**
5. The coach will see your decision on their Expenses page

> Mileage is calculated automatically at the HMRC rate of 45p per mile.

---

## Your Own Clock In / Out

You clock in the same way coaches do.

1. Tap **Clock In / Out**
2. Select the school you're at (you'll see all schools since you have area access)
3. Tap **Clock In**
4. Tap **Clock Out** when your session ends

If you're at a location that isn't listed, you can choose **Other / Custom Location** and type it in.

---

## Submitting Your Own Expenses

1. Tap **Expenses** from the home screen
2. Tap **Submit Expense**
3. Choose the type:
   - **Mileage** — enter miles, amount calculates automatically at 45p/mile
   - **Travel** — trains, buses, parking etc. — enter the amount manually
   - **Other** — anything else with a receipt
4. Fill in the date and description
5. Tap **Submit**

Your director will then approve or reject it.

---

## Your Digital ID Card

All ASO staff have a digital ID card in the app. Keep it up to date — it shows your DBS status and certificate expiry dates.

1. Tap **My Profile** from the home screen
2. Tap **Upload Photo** to add your face
3. Fill in your DBS number and expiry dates
4. Add any coaching certificates using the **Add** button in the certificates section
5. Tap **Save Details**

Your ID card is at the top of your profile page — you can show it on your phone to schools if needed.

---

## Distributing This App to Your Coaches

Once Naima has created accounts for your coaches, here's how to get them started:

1. **Send them the app link** via WhatsApp or text
2. **Include their login details** (email + password)
3. **Share the coach guide** (separate PDF/document)
4. Ask them to **install it to their home screen** before their first session
5. Ask them to **update their profile** with their photo and DBS details

> Most coaches get the hang of it in about 5 minutes. The main things they need to do are clock in/out and keep their profile updated.
